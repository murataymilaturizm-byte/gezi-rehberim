# WhatsApp Business Setup for Travel Agencies: Step-by-Step Guide (2026)

For agencies operating in Türkiye, the de facto main channel of tour sales is no longer the phone or e-mail: it is **WhatsApp**. The customer sees your tour on Instagram, asks their question on WhatsApp, and confirms the deposit with a bank receipt sent on WhatsApp. Yet many agencies still run the business on a personal number with a chaotic chat list — and every chaotic day means lost customers.

This guide is for newly founded agencies and for those who want to bring their WhatsApp traffic under control: the differences between regular WhatsApp, the WhatsApp Business app and the WhatsApp Business API (Platform), step-by-step setup, agency-specific usage patterns, the Meta rules you must know, and the point at which you should switch to automation. A 12-question FAQ closes the guide.

> **In short:** For a new agency the right start is the **WhatsApp Business app** (free) on a number opened in the company's name. With the profile, catalogue, quick replies and labels in place, even one person can run an orderly sales line. When volume grows to the "we can't keep up" point, you move to the **WhatsApp Business API** plus an AI assistant layer — bookings are then taken automatically, 24/7.

## Three Different WhatsApps: Which One Is Right for Your Agency?

| | Regular WhatsApp | WhatsApp Business (App) | WhatsApp Business API (Platform) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Who it's for** | Personal use | Small business, one or a few people | Businesses that want automation and scale |
| **Cost** | Free | Free | Meta message/template fees + solution provider |
| **Business profile** | No | Yes (address, hours, catalogue) | Yes (verified business account) |
| **Automation** | No | Basic (greeting, away message) | Full (chatbot, integrations, multi-user) |
| **Green verification badge** | No | Limited | Possible via application |
| **Meaning for an agency** | Avoid | **The right start** | **The growth stage** |

Two rules up front. **First**, don't run agency business on your personal number — customer messages mix into private life, the number leaves with you when staff change, and you can't present a business profile. **Second**, the same number cannot be on the Business app and the API at the same time; when you move to the API, the number binds to the platform. So choose your number as "the number that will one day move to the API".

## Step by Step: Setting Up WhatsApp Business

### 1. Start with the right number
Get a new line in the company's name (a landline can also be verified, but a mobile number is more flexible in practice). You will use this single number everywhere from business cards to the Instagram bio — changing the number later means resetting your customers' memory.

### 2. Install the app and complete the profile fully
Install WhatsApp Business, verify the number, and fill in the business profile **leaving no field empty**: agency title (consistent with your TÜRSAB title), category ("Travel Agency"), address, working hours, website, short description. Use your logo as the profile photo — the customer must see the answer to "who am I writing to?" in the first second. An incomplete profile raises the "is this a real business?" doubt in new customers; a complete one is your first trust signal.

### 3. Catalogue: put your tours in the shop window
The catalogue feature lets you list tours with image + price + description. For each tour: a cover image, a clear title ("Cappadocia Balloon Tour — 2 Days 1 Night"), per-person price, a short programme. Instead of typing "here are our tours" again and again, you send the catalogue link; the customer browses the shop window even at 2 a.m. Keep it current — a catalogue showing last season's price costs you trust.

### 4. Quick replies: template your 10 most frequent answers
Set up canned texts triggered by shortcuts like "/price", "/cancel", "/deposit". The most repeated agency questions are well known: price, dates, departure point, what's included, deposit-refund terms, child rate. Write these 8-10 answers well once; stop typing them dozens of times a day. Tip: end each answer with the next step — "The deposit is 30%. Which date shall we reserve for you?" A message that answers is good; a message that moves the sale forward is better.

### 5. Labels: turn your chat list into a CRM
Define labels like "New customer", "Awaiting deposit", "Booking confirmed", "Call after season" and label every chat. Even in a one-person agency, a week later the answer to "who was at which stage?" lives in the labels. Label discipline is also the rehearsal for the real CRM you will eventually adopt.

### 6. Automated messages: greeting and after-hours
Set up the greeting message (for first-time writers) and the away message (outside working hours). Be honest in the away message and manage expectations: "We've received your message; we reply by 09:30 tomorrow at the latest." An empty promise ("we'll reply right away") that isn't kept destroys trust. Note: these automations only manage the *first touch* — they cannot answer questions or take bookings. The "any room for 4 people tomorrow?" message that arrives at night waits unanswered until morning.

### 7. Put your WhatsApp link everywhere
Place your direct wa.me/90XXXXXXXXXX link in the Instagram bio, on the Google Business profile, on your website and in your ads. Every step that reduces the number of clicks between you and the customer increases conversion.

## Agency-Specific Usage Patterns

- **Receipt confirmation routine:** Deposit receipts arrive on WhatsApp. Standardise the trio "receipt received → label changed → confirmation sent"; one missed receipt is one cancelled holiday.
- **Pre-tour reminder:** Meeting point and time, 24 hours before departure. If you do it by hand, set a calendar — a forgotten reminder means 6 a.m. phone calls.
- **Post-tour message:** Thanks + a short satisfaction question + a Google review request. The cheapest marketing a new agency has is yesterday's guest's review today.
- **Seasonal re-contact:** "You joined our Cappadocia tour last summer; early-booking discounts are now open" — the old-customer list you labelled is the first sales source of every season.

## Meta's Rules: Don't Grow Blind

- **The 24-hour window:** A business may write freely within 24 hours of the customer's last message. After the window closes (especially on the API side) only **approved template messages** can be sent. Sending bulk announcements without knowing this rule ends with undelivered messages.
- **Spam and block signals:** Unsolicited messages to many people in a short time, user blocks and complaints can get your number restricted. Before bulk messaging a list, make sure the recipients know you and an opt-out is available.
- **Business verification:** Verification via Meta Business Manager is the gateway both to the trust badge and to the API world. Keep your commercial documents (tax plate, registry) ready.

## The "We Can't Keep Up" Moment: Moving to the API + an AI Assistant

The WhatsApp Business app is enough for an agency that fits 10-20 chats into a day. But if these symptoms have started, the ceiling is near:

- When you run ads, messages arrive **faster than you can answer**,
- Questions arriving at night and on weekends wait unanswered until morning,
- Foreign customers write in their own language and the flow breaks,
- You copy-paste the same answers dozens of times a day,
- The team can't share the line because it lives in one app on one phone.

At this point the solution is an AI booking assistant built on the WhatsApp Business **API**. The difference is this: the app's automated messages only greet; an assistant on the API does **the work itself** — it explains the tour, shows available dates, collects party size, name and phone, records the booking, sends deposit details, and sends the pre-tour reminder and post-tour satisfaction survey by itself. It answers in Turkish to customers writing Turkish and in Russian to those writing Russian. You watch bookings flow in the panel; if you want to step in, you pause the bot with one tap and take over the chat.

The switch is easier than assumed: your existing number moves to the API (chat history stays in the app; the number and the customers' perception don't change), your tours are entered into the panel, and the assistant starts answering the same day. On the cost side there are Meta's message/template fees and the solution provider's subscription — weigh them against the price of a single missed group booking.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**1. Can I move my personal number to WhatsApp Business?**
Yes, an existing number can be moved to the Business app and the chat history is preserved. Still, opening a separate business number from the start is healthier in the long run.

**2. Is WhatsApp Business paid?**
The app is completely free. Charging exists only on the API (Platform) side: Meta's message/template fees vary by country and message type; check the current tariff on Meta's official page.

**3. Can I use the same number on two phones?**
The Business app offers limited multi-device via linked devices. Real multi-user work (a team on the same line, permission separation) is the API side's job.

**4. How do I get the green verification badge?**
The badge depends on Meta's business verification and notability criteria; on the API side it is evaluated via application. The badge increases trust but is not a precondition of sales — a complete profile and fast replies sell more than a badge does.

**5. How many tours can I list in the catalogue?**
Far more than you will practically need; the real issue is freshness, not count. Remove tours not on sale, and update the price the same day it changes.

**6. Does the 24-hour rule also apply on the app side?**
The rule is in essence the framework of all business messaging; in practice it bites hardest on the API side (outside the window only approved templates go out). On the app side too, bulk/unsolicited sends made without waiting for the customer to write produce spam signals.

**7. Is bulk broadcasting safe?**
To customers who have saved you and talked to you, at reasonable intervals, with valuable content: yes. To purchased lists: you put your number at risk.

**8. Is keeping customer data on WhatsApp a problem under Turkish data-protection law (KVKK)?**
Customer communication can be conducted on a legitimate basis; your duty to inform about personal-data processing continues. Keep your KVKK notices on your website and in the booking process; for data security, mind screen locks and backup discipline on business devices.

**9. Can I keep using the WhatsApp app after moving to the API?**
Not on the same number — a number is either on the app or on the API. After the switch, chats move to the panel interface; a good panel offers everything you were used to in the app (chat list, takeover, history) and more.

**10. Will an AI assistant give wrong information?**
Quality systems read tour data from the panel you fill in, and rather than inventing an answer on unknown topics they forward the request to you. Before buying, deliberately ask the assistant hard questions: a non-existent tour, an incomplete phone number, an off-scope request — the answers show the system's maturity.

**11. How long does API setup take?**
If your business verification is ready, you can go live within the same day to a few days once the number is connected and tour data entered. The longest item is usually Meta business verification; prepare the documents early.

**12. I'm a new agency — should I start with the API from day one?**
If your message volume is low, starting with the Business app is reasonable. But if you will launch with ads — that is, if you expect heavy traffic from day one — starting with the assistant keeps your ad budget from melting into unanswered messages.
