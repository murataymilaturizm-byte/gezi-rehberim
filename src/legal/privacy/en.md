## 1. Data We Collect

To provide our services, we collect the following personal data:

- **Name and surname** — for reservations and communication
- **Phone number** — for WhatsApp communication

- **WhatsApp message content** — for tour consulting and reservation management

- **Language and currency preferences** — for personalised service
- **Device and usage data** — to improve service quality (anonymous)

## 2. How We Use Your Data

The data we collect is used solely for the following purposes:

- Creating and managing tour reservations
- Sending tour reminders and follow-up messages
- Providing customer support services
- Analysing and improving service quality
- Fulfilling legal obligations

Your data is used for **direct marketing** only with your explicit consent.

## 3. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your data on the following legal grounds:

- **Contractual necessity:** To fulfil our service agreement
- **Legitimate interests:** To improve service quality
- **Legal obligation:** To comply with applicable law
- **Consent:** For marketing communications

## 4. Third-Party Sharing

Your personal data is shared with third parties only in the following cases:

- **Meta / WhatsApp Business API:** Communication infrastructure
- **Secure payment providers:** For payment processing (card details are never stored by us)
- **Supabase (database infrastructure):** For secure data storage

Your data is **never sold, rented or shared commercially with third parties**.

## 5. International Data Transfers

Our infrastructure providers may process data in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the United States. Such transfers are carried out with appropriate safeguards under GDPR (Standard Contractual Clauses etc.).

## 6. Retention Period

Your data is retained for the duration of our service relationship and subsequently for a maximum of **5 years** under legal obligations. Earlier deletion is possible upon request.

## 7. Your Rights

Under GDPR and applicable data protection law, you have the following rights:

- **Right of access:** Request access to your data
- **Right to rectification:** Request correction of inaccurate data
- **Right to erasure:** Request deletion of your data ([/data-deletion](/data-deletion))
- **Right to data portability:** Receive your data in machine-readable format ([/data-export](/data-export))
- **Right to object:** Object to data processing
- **Right to restriction:** Request restriction of processing

Our website uses strictly necessary cookies and, subject to your consent, analytics and marketing cookies. You can manage your preferences via the cookie banner.

## 9. Security

Your data is protected by encryption, access controls and regular security audits. In the event of a data breach, you will be notified within the legally required timeframes.
